# Copying other stacks' skills into this repo

**Decision:** we do not vendor skills from pstack, tstack or Matt Pocock's
pack. Matt's pack is offered as a pass-through marketplace entry that installs
from his repo.

**Why:** a vendored copy drifts from upstream, and its fixes never reach you.
Where we wanted an idea, we wrote our own skill and credited the source in
[CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md).
